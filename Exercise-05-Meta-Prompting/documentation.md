# Exercise 5 — Meta-Prompting

## Objective

Use an AI model to generate a more detailed prompt from a high-level request.

## User's original meta-prompt

> Act as a beginner friendly football teacher and generate a detailed yet specific prompt which covers more about all the basics of football, formations, leagues, club competitions and international competitions and tactics

## Model used

Gemini.

## Generated prompt characteristics

Gemini generated a structured prompt with the role:

> Expert, patient, and engaging football (soccer) coach and teacher.

The target audience was a complete beginner. The generated prompt requested easy-to-understand teaching, analogies and real-world examples.

It organized the subject into five pillars:

1. **Basics:** Rules, offside, fouls, cards, scoring, match duration and positions.
2. **Formations and tactics:** 4-3-3, 4-4-2, possession, counter-attacking, high press and defensive block.
3. **Major European leagues:** The Big Five leagues and their styles.
4. **Club competitions:** League versus knockout formats, Champions League, Europa League and domestic cups such as the FA Cup.
5. **International competitions:** National teams, World Cup, Euro and Copa América.

The generated prompt also emphasized simple language, analogies and real-world examples.

## Evaluation

- The generated prompt preserved the broad scope of the original request.
- It made the learning areas more structured.
- It specified a beginner audience and a teaching role.
- It converted a broad request into a more usable prompt.

## Concepts demonstrated

- Meta-prompting: asking an AI to create or improve a prompt.
- Role specification.
- Audience definition.
- Scope organization.
- Specific instructions and output expectations.

## Conclusion

The exercise demonstrated how a broad instruction can be transformed into a structured beginner-learning prompt through meta-prompting.
