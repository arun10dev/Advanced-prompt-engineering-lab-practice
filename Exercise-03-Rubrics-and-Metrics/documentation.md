# Exercise 3 — Rubrics & Metrics

## Objective

Create a weighted rubric to evaluate an AI-generated smartphone review and calculate an aggregate score.

## Scenario

Generate a detailed smartphone review from the perspective of a customer who owns the phone, is a mobile gamer and YouTube gameplay streamer.

The exercise used a Samsung Galaxy A56 5G and an exercise price of ₹40,000.

## Specifications supplied

- Display: 6.7-inch Super AMOLED.
- Resolution: FHD+ (2340 × 1080).
- Refresh rate: 120 Hz.
- Processor: Exynos 1580.
- Rear cameras: 50 MP + 12 MP + 5 MP.
- Front camera: 12 MP.
- Battery: 5,000 mAh.
- Wired charging: 45 W.
- RAM: 8 GB.
- Storage: 256 GB.
- Software: Android 15 with One UI 7.
- Build: glass front and back with an aluminium frame.
- Protection: IP67.

## Rubric

Six criteria were assigned 20 marks each.

| Criterion | Maximum marks |
|---|---:|
| Display | 20 |
| Performance | 20 |
| Camera | 20 |
| Battery | 20 |
| Design | 20 |
| Storage | 20 |
| **Total** | **120** |

The value-for-money question—“Is it worth ₹40,000?”—was included as an additional concluding assessment, not as a seventh scoring criterion.

## Classification scale supplied for the exercise

| Score range | Classification |
|---|---|
| Above 100 | Excellent |
| Between 90 and 100 | Very Good |
| Between 80 and 90 | Good |
| Between 70 and 80 | Average |
| Between 50 and 70 | Bad |
| Between 30 and 50 | Very Bad |
| Less than 30 | Worst |

The original scale is preserved as supplied. The exercise used six criteria × 20 marks = 120 total marks.

## AI-generated review scores

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---:|
| Display | 17/20 |
| Performance | 14/20 |
| Camera | 12/20 |
| Battery | 16/20 |
| Design | 15/20 |
| Storage | 14/20 |
| **Total** | **88/120** |

Calculation: 17 + 14 + 12 + 16 + 15 + 14 = **88**.

Under the supplied classification scale, 88 falls in the **Good** band (between 80 and 90).

## Value-for-money assessment

The generated review concluded that the phone was worth ₹40,000 for the described gamer/streamer use case.

This was the AI review's conclusion under the exercise scenario, not an independently established market-value verdict.

## Findings

- A rubric translates broad quality expectations into explicit criteria.
- Weights make the contribution of each criterion visible.
- The aggregate score summarizes the individual criterion scores.
- A classification scale gives the aggregate score an interpretable label.
- The review was intentionally requested from an owner/gamer/streamer perspective; that was part of the task design.

## Concepts demonstrated

- Rubric design.
- Criterion weighting.
- Scoring.
- Aggregate metric calculation.
- Score interpretation using classification bands.

## Conclusion

The AI-generated review scored **88/120**, classified as **Good** under the user's scale. The exercise demonstrated how a rubric and metric can make an evaluation more structured.
